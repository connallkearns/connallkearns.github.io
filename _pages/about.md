I'm a Data Science graduate with a background in Biomedical Sciences, interested in applying computational and mathematical approaches to complex problems in biology and medicine.

I hold an MSc in Data Science, with Distinction, from the University of Sussex, and a BSc in Biomedical Sciences from Maastricht University. My master's dissertation focused on probabilistic modelling of neurodegenerative disease, combining my interest in biomedical research with computational modelling.

My research interests include computational biology, machine learning, probabilistic modelling, and scientific computing.
